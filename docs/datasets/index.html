Dataset Sources

Public datasets used in the UAV sound detection and anomaly-detection experiments, together with project-collected field recordings.

← Back to the project website

Multiclass Drone Audio

Recordings of several drone types and background sound categories. Used for the main UAV anomaly-detection experiments.

Open source dataset ↗

Binary Drone Audio

Labeled drone and non-drone recordings used for binary audio-detection experiments.

Open on Kaggle ↗

AeroSonicDB

Low-flying aircraft, silence, and urban environmental recordings used to test transfer to other aerial sounds.

Open on Kaggle ↗

MIMII

Normal and anomalous industrial-machine sounds used as an additional anomaly-detection benchmark. Reported experiments currently use the slider subset.

Open on Zenodo ↗

Project Field Recordings

Outdoor DJI UAV and background recordings collected by the project team with multiple microphones, flight paths, and recording conditions.

Not publicly distributed

Supplemental Online Audio

Additional exploratory FPV, UAV, and environmental recordings used during data investigation and labeling.
