Public sources

External datasets

The original datasets remain hosted by their publishers and are not redistributed here.

Binary Drone Audio

Labeled drone and non-drone recordings used as a public binary benchmark.

Open on Kaggle ↗

Multiclass Drone Audio

A public drone-audio corpus with multiple drone and background categories.

Open source dataset ↗

AeroSonicDB

Low-flying aircraft rather than small UAVs; included as a related aerial-acoustics benchmark.

Open on Zenodo ↗

Licensing note

AeroSonicDB is distributed under CC BY-NC 4.0. A combined corpus containing it therefore carries a non-commercial restriction.

Team-recorded data

Field datasets

Electric UAVs, FPV, and UAV were recorded by the project team under real outdoor conditions and cover distinct platform families.

Electric UAVs

Electric UAV field recordings and non-UAV outdoor audio. The final mini-SE-Net run for this dataset used augmented training data.

FPV

FPV platform recordings paired with a broad background class. This dataset is also the target of the cross-dataset transfer experiment.

UAV

The largest field corpus and the hardest single dataset in the embedding benchmark, with best AUC 0.759.

Recording conditions

Outdoor recordings vary in platform, distance, flight path, wind, terrain, microphone placement, and background activity.

Positive examples contain rotor noise from the relevant platform family; negatives span ambient outdoor scenes and non-UAV sources. The paper is the source of truth for final split counts and evaluation details.
