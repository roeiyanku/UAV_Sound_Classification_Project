External sources

Public datasets used in experiments

The original datasets remain hosted by their publishers.

Multiclass Drone Audio

Several drone types and background sound categories used for the main UAV anomaly-detection experiments.

Open source dataset ↗

Binary Drone Audio

Labeled drone and non-drone recordings used for binary audio-detection experiments.

Open on Kaggle ↗

AeroSonicDB

Low-flying aircraft, silence, and urban recordings used to test transfer to other aerial sounds.

Open on Kaggle ↗

MIMII

Normal and anomalous industrial-machine sounds used as an additional anomaly-detection benchmark.

Open on Zenodo ↗

Project Field Recordings

Outdoor DJI UAV and background recordings collected with multiple microphones and flight conditions.

Not publicly distributed

Supplemental Online Audio

Additional exploratory FPV, UAV, and environmental recordings used during investigation and labeling.
